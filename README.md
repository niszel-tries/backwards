a mini game ekekek

STORYLINE PLAN:

PART 1 — THE CELLAR

Your eyes slowly open.

Cold concrete presses against your back.

Sarah: “...Where am I?”

The room is dark.

The air is stale, and the smell of damp concrete fills the room.

You slowly sit up.

There is a single door in front of you.

You try to open it.

Locked.

Then...

Footsteps.

The footsteps grow louder.

Someone is coming.

A voice suddenly comes from the other side of the room.

Voice: “Sarah?”

Voice: “Hello? Are you there?”

You freeze.

That voice...

You know it.

Sarah: “...James?”

A figure steps closer.

James: “Oh my God. Sarah. I finally found you.”

Sarah: “James! Where are we? Where am I?!”

James immediately raises a finger to his lips.

James: “Shhhhh. Sarah. Keep your voice down.”

He looks toward the hallway.

James: “He's going to hear us.”

Sarah: “Who...?”

James: “I'll explain later. We need to get out of here.”

He pulls out a pair of bolt cutters.

James: “Just wait here.”

James disappears through the doorway.

You hear metal scraping against the floor.

CLANK.

CLANK.

A few moments later, James returns.

He kneels beside the door.

CLANK.

The chain finally gives way.

James stands.

James: “Come on. We need to move before—”

He suddenly stops.

His expression changes.

James looks past you.

James: “Sarah...”

---

PART 2 — THE ROOM

Sarah: “What? What's with your fa—”

Before you can finish, James grabs your hand.

James: “Wait. Don't look.”

He pulls you down the hallway.

You barely have time to keep up.

James suddenly turns into a random room.

He pulls you inside.

The door closes behind you.

Click.

You look around.

You have no idea where you are.

James stands beside the door.

He listens carefully.

Silence.

James finally lets out a quiet breath.

---

PART 3 — THE CABINET

James finally lets go of your hand.

Sarah: “What are you doing?”

Sarah: “Where are we?”

Sarah: “And what are we even running from?”

James looks at you for a moment.

James: “I know you'll start being loud again when I let go of you.”

He chuckles quietly.

James: “You haven't changed, Sarah.”

You stare at him.

Before you can respond, your shoulder bumps against something.

THUD.

You turn around.

A large wooden cabinet stands against the wall.

Its surface is covered in dust.

Sarah: “What's that?”

James looks at it.

James: “I don't know.”

You look at the cabinet again.

The room is quiet.

No footsteps.

No voices.

You hesitate.

Part of you wants to open it.

Another part of you thinks...

What if something comes out?

You stare at the cabinet.

CHOICE:

OPEN THE CABINET

LEAVE IT ALONE

---

IF SARAH OPENS IT:

You slowly open the cabinet.

Creak.

Nothing happens.

Inside, among the old boxes, you notice something.

An old key.

You reach inside and pick it up.

ITEM ACQUIRED: OLD KEY

Sarah: “A key...?”

James looks at it.

James: “Keep it. Might be useful.”

Sarah puts it away.

James turns toward the door.

James: “Let's go.”

---

IF SARAH LEAVES IT:

You stare at the cabinet for another moment.

Then you slowly step away.

Sarah: “No... Let's just go.”

James nods.

James: “Yeah.”

He turns toward the door.

James: “Let's go.”

---

PART 4 — THE HALLWAY

Sarah and James step out of the room.

The hallway is longer than Sarah expected.

The lights overhead flicker occasionally, but none of them seem completely dead.

Sarah looks around.

Sarah: “What's up?”

James keeps walking.

James: “What?”

Sarah: “You've been acting weird since you found me.”

James glances at her.

James: “Well, you did wake up in a locked room with no idea where you were.”

Sarah frowns.

Sarah: “That's not an answer.”

James: “I know.”

He gives her a small smile before looking ahead again.

Sarah follows him.

Sarah: “Seriously. What's going on?”

James slows down.

James: “I told you. I'll explain.”

Sarah: “When?”

James: “When we're somewhere safer.”

Sarah: “That's what you've been saying.”

James sighs.

James: “Because every time I try to explain something, you ask another question.”

Sarah looks at him.

Sarah: “Maybe because you're terrible at explaining things.”

James lets out a quiet laugh.

James: “Yeah. Maybe.”

They continue walking.

After a few moments, Sarah speaks again.

Sarah: “And why are you acting like this anyway?”

James looks at her.

James: “You're asking me that?”

Sarah: “Yes.”

James smiles slightly.

James: “You're the weird one, Sarah.”

Sarah: “Excuse me?”

James turns away and continues walking.

James: “You can't even remember anything.”

Sarah's expression changes.

Sarah: “...What are you talking about?”

James doesn't stop.

James: “Exactly.”

Sarah: “James.”

He keeps walking.

Sarah follows him, now even more confused.

James eventually slows down.

James: “Look, I'm not trying to mess with you.”

His expression becomes a little more serious.

James: “I know this is confusing.”

He looks down the hallway.

James: “But right now, the less noise we make, the better.”

Sarah looks away.

Sarah: “Fine.”

James smiles.

James: “There. See? You're learning.”

Sarah: “Don't push it.”

James chuckles.

---

They eventually reach an intersection.

But this time, there isn't just one way forward.

There are three hallways.

LEFT

A narrow hallway with old wooden flooring.

Most of the lights are broken.

Only a faint light can be seen somewhere farther down.

There are several doors along the walls.

Some are slightly open.

Others are completely shut.

At the very end is a large window.

Something has been written across the glass, but it's too far away to read.

---

RIGHT

A long concrete hallway.

The lights here are still working.

It's brighter than the other two paths.

There are no doors.

No windows.

Nothing on the walls.

Just a straight path disappearing around a corner.

It looks like the safest route.

Almost too safe.

---

STRAIGHT

A darker hallway.

The ceiling is lower here, and old pipes run along the walls.

There is a faint dripping sound somewhere ahead.

Drip.

...

Drip.

...

Drip.

At the end of the hallway is a heavy metal door.

Unlike the other doors, it has a small circular slot in the middle.

---

James looks at all three paths.

James: “Well...”

Sarah looks at him.

Sarah: “What?”

James points toward the hallways.

James: “Three choices.”

Sarah looks between them.

Sarah: “And you don't know which one?”

James shakes his head.

James: “Nope.”

Sarah looks at him suspiciously.

Sarah: “You're telling me you've been here and you don't know?”

James gives her a look.

James: “Sarah, I spent most of my time trying not to get caught.”

He gestures toward the three paths.

James: “I didn't exactly have time to make a map.”

Sarah laughs quietly despite herself.

Sarah: “Fair.”

James looks at the hallways again.

James: “We don't know what's ahead.”

He pauses.

James: “So think before you choose.”

Sarah studies the three paths.

WHERE DO YOU GO?

LEFT HALLWAY

RIGHT HALLWAY

STRAIGHT HALLWAY

---

IF SARAH CHOOSES LEFT

Sarah looks down the narrow hallway.

The broken lights make it difficult to see what lies ahead.

The half-open doors bother her.

Sarah: “I don't like this one.”

James looks toward the hallway.

James: “Neither do I.”

Sarah looks at him.

Sarah: “Then why would we go this way?”

James shrugs.

James: “Because sometimes the path you don't like is the one worth checking.”

Sarah looks at the distant window.

Sarah: “And what's that?”

James: “No idea.”

He pauses.

James: “Want to find out?”

Sarah hesitates.

Then steps forward.

James follows.

---

IF SARAH CHOOSES RIGHT

Sarah looks down the bright hallway.

Sarah: “This one looks safest.”

James studies it.

James: “It does.”

Sarah: “But?”

James looks at her.

James: “You noticed it too.”

Sarah looks back at the hallway.

Sarah: “It's too clean.”

James nods.

James: “Exactly.”

Sarah takes a breath.

Sarah: “Still... I'd rather see where it goes.”

James smiles.

James: “Fair enough.”

They begin walking.

---

IF SARAH CHOOSES STRAIGHT

Sarah looks toward the dark hallway.

The dripping continues.

Drip.

Drip.

Drip.

Her eyes settle on the metal door at the end.

Sarah: “That one.”

James looks at her.

James: “You sure?”

Sarah nods.

Sarah: “I don't know.”

James smiles.

James: “Honest answer.”

They begin walking toward it.

As they get closer, Sarah notices the circular slot.

She reaches into her pocket.

Her fingers touch the old metal token.

She looks at James.

James notices.

He raises an eyebrow.

James: “Looks like we found out what that's for.”

Sarah pulls out the token.

Sarah: “You knew?”

James shakes his head.

James: “I had a guess.”

Sarah smiles slightly.

Sarah: “Of course you did.”

She turns toward the door.

The token fits perfectly into the circular slot.

CLICK.

The lock releases.

Sarah looks at James.

James looks back at her.

Neither of them speaks.

Then—

The door slowly opens.

END OF PART 4
