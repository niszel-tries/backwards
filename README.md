STORYLINE PLAN

PART 1 — THE CELLAR

Opening

Your eyes slowly open.

Cold concrete presses against your back.

The room is dark.

The air is stale, and the smell of damp concrete fills the room.

You slowly sit up.

Sarah: “...Where am I?”

There is a single door in front of you.

You try to open it.

Locked.

Then...

Footsteps.

The footsteps grow louder.

Someone is coming.

A voice suddenly comes from the other side of the room.

Voice: “Sarah?”

Voice: “Hello? Are you there?”

You freeze.

That voice...

You know it.

Sarah: “...James?”

A figure steps closer.

James: “Oh my God. Sarah. I finally found you.”

Sarah: “James! Where are we? Where am I?!”

James immediately raises a finger to his lips.

James: “Shhhhh. Sarah. Keep your voice down.”

He looks toward the hallway.

James: “He's going to hear us.”

Sarah: “Who...?”

James: “I'll explain later. We need to get out of here.”

He pulls out a pair of bolt cutters.

James: “Just wait here.”

James disappears through the doorway.

You hear metal scraping against the floor.

CLANK.

CLANK.

A few moments later, James returns.

He kneels beside the door.

CLANK.

The chain finally gives way.

James stands.

James: “Come on. We need to move before—”

He suddenly stops.

His expression changes.

James looks past you.

James: “Sarah...”

---

PART 2 — THE ROOM

Sarah: “What? What's with your fa—”

Before you can finish, James grabs your hand.

James: “Wait. Don't look.”

He pulls you down the hallway.

You barely have time to keep up.

James suddenly turns into a random room.

He pulls you inside.

The door closes behind you.

Click.

You look around.

You have no idea where you are.

James stands beside the door.

He listens carefully.

Silence.

James finally lets out a quiet breath.

---

PART 3 — THE CABINET

James finally lets go of your hand.

Sarah: “What are you doing?”

Sarah: “Where are we?”

Sarah: “And what are we even running from?”

James looks at you for a moment.

James: “I know you'll start being loud again when I let go of you.”

He chuckles quietly.

James: “You haven't changed, Sarah.”

You stare at him.

Before you can respond, your shoulder bumps against something.

THUD.

You turn around.

A large wooden cabinet stands against the wall.

Its surface is covered in dust.

Sarah: “What's that?”

James looks at it.

James: “I don't know.”

You look at the cabinet again.

The room is quiet.

No footsteps.

No voices.

You hesitate.

Part of you wants to open it.

Another part of you thinks...

What if something comes out?

You stare at the cabinet.

CHOICE

- OPEN THE CABINET
- LEAVE IT ALONE

An old key.

James: “Let's go.”

PART 4 — THE INTERSECTION

Sarah and James eventually reach an intersection.

There are three hallways.

James looks at them carefully.

Sarah looks at him suspiciously.

Sarah: “You're telling me you've been here and you don't know?”

James gives her a look.

James: “Sarah, I spent most of my time trying not to get caught.”

He gestures toward the three paths.

James: “I didn't exactly have time to make a map.”

Sarah laughs quietly despite herself.

Sarah: “Fair.”

James looks at the hallways again.

James: “We don't know what's ahead.”

He pauses.

James: “So think before you choose.”

Sarah studies the three paths.

STORY STRUCTURE / IMPORTANT GAME ELEMENTS

Main Branches

BRIGHT HALLWAY

- Looks safest.
- Leads to the frightening footsteps.
- James and Sarah are forced back to the intersection.
- Can later become dangerous again when Sarah is alone.

DRIPPING HALLWAY

- Darker and more suspicious-looking.
- Contains the metal door.
- The metal token unlocks the door.
- Leads outside into the forest.
- Leads to the road and the vehicle choices.
- Leads to the boyfriend/memory reveal.

WINDOW HALLWAY

- Old wooden floor.
- Broken lights.
- Multiple doors.
- Window at the end.
- Window appears to be an exit but cannot be opened.
- Creepy message on the glass.
- A closed door begins opening.
- Leads to the monster encounter.
- Can branch into the Bright or Dripping hallway.

Items

OLD KEY

Obtained by opening the cabinet in Part 3.

ITEM ACQUIRED: OLD KEY

James: “Keep it. Might be useful.”

METAL TOKEN

Used to unlock the circular slot on the metal door.

The token is already in Sarah's possession when she reaches the door.

Important Story Reveals

- Sarah has memory loss.
- Sarah initially believes James is simply someone she knows.
- James knows Sarah better than she realizes.
- James knows she has forgotten something.
- James is actually Sarah's boyfriend.
- Sarah has forgotten not only the location and events, but James himself.
- Photos and old messages later confirm their relationship.
- Something strange can be seen in one of the photos after Sarah returns home.

Recurring / Suspicious Details

- “He's going to hear us.”
- James knows Sarah will be loud.
- James says, “You haven't changed, Sarah.”
- The yellow car's driver has a bandage covering part of their face.
- The old vehicle has strange dents.
- Jim is James's favorite pizza delivery guy.
- The window has an unknown message.
- Something is present behind James during the window-hallway encounter.
- Something appears in the background of one of Sarah and James's photos.

NOTE: "[THE MESSAGE — TBD]", the exact monster design/identity, the purpose of the OLD KEY, and the meaning of the yellow car/bandage are intentionally left undecided.

NOTE: The story currently has some routes that converge into other routes. This is preserved exactly as planned rather than changing the branching structure.
